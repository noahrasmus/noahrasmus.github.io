Hi, I'm Noah.

I tattoo in black and grey — mostly figures, animals and objects drawn with hard contrast so they still read from across a room in ten years' time.

Most pieces start as a conversation. Bring a rough idea, a photograph, or nothing at all; I'll draw it and we'll change it together before any needle comes out. I keep the calendar deliberately short so nothing gets rushed.

If you've never been tattooed before, say so. It changes nothing about the work and everything about how I'll walk you through the day.
