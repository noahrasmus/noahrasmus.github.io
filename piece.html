Warrior sleeve

Custom black and grey realism sleeve by Noah Rasmus, tattooing from Camp Hill Collective in Camp Hill, Pennsylvania.

We started from a single reference and drew the rest onto the leg so the figure follows the calf rather than fighting it. The skull at the ankle came last, once we could see how much room was left.
